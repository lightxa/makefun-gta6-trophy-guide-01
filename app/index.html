        About this guide

        Leonida Ledger is an original, offline guide. It shows the 55 official PlayStation 5 trophies exactly as PlayStation publishes them — names, descriptions and grades — and adds a guide in our own words for each one, with the sources its facts come from named beside it. No Rockstar logo, artwork or game screenshot is used anywhere; the look is our own.

        Where the facts come from

        • Official names, descriptions and grades: the Exophase trophy list and the PSNProfiles trophy list for Grand Theft Auto VI.
        • How-to detail: the PowerPyx GTA 6 Trophy Guide and the PlayStationTrophies.org GTA 6 guide, cited per trophy. Their words are never copied — every guide here is rewritten for this page.
        • Counts (42 Shooting Range challenges, 14 drone races, 100 WAiNK locations, 52 areas and more) are the counts PlayStation tracks, as listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles.

        How progress is stored

        Your ticks live in this browser only (localStorage). Export writes a JSON file you can keep or move to another machine; Import reads it back. Nothing is uploaded, and the page makes no network request at all.

        GTA 6 has no online trophies in its official list, so the Online filter is empty by design — if a future list adds one, it will appear there.
